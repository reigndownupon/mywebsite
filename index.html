01 — The center

Everything here comes back to one question: does this help us love God and people better?

I spent years in ministry, and today much of my work and learning lives in cybersecurity and AI. Those worlds may look far apart, but they keep bringing me back to the same place: Scripture matters, truth matters, and the person standing in front of us matters. You do not have to be an expert or have all the right words before you are welcome here.

Here is the thing: every tool, every security choice, and every piece of AI work eventually lands in somebody's life. People are not off to the side of the work. They are the reason to do it well.
