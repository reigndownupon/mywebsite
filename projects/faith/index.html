01 — Why fAIth

Helping people see Jesus clearly and read Scripture in context.

I started fAIth because Scripture can be flattened, pulled away from its setting, and used in ways that miss the people, questions, and story already inside the text. Better context does not answer every question for us, but it can help us slow down, see more clearly, and handle the Bible with greater care.

The goal is not to tell people what the Bible has to mean. The goal is to help them see what is there, understand the world around it, and keep reading with Jesus at the center.
02 — Reader experience

Context at the depth the reader chooses.

Hover

Notice without leaving the verse

A short cue explains a useful word, setting, or connection while the reader stays in the passage.

Guided

Context that follows the reading

A companion view moves with the passage and offers more detail without taking over the page.

Research

A deeper place to keep learning

Longer context brings together passage flow, background, language, related Scripture, and sources.

03 — How it works

Bible first, evidence attached, people still responsible.

  1. Begin with the biblical passage, its literary setting, and the questions the text is actually asking.
  2. Gather historical, cultural, language, and related-passage context from sources that can be used honestly.
  3. Keep direct evidence separate from inference, and make confidence and review status visible.
  4. Review every reader-facing explanation for accuracy, tone, rights, and pastoral care.
  5. Let the reader choose how much context to open, then return attention to Scripture.
04 — Current work

What I am building now.

01

Luke 15 as the flagship

The lost sheep, lost coin, both sons, and the welcoming father are shaping the clearest version of the reader experience.

02

Focused passage experiences

John 4 and Matthew 5 are helping test how cultural setting, literary flow, and care notes belong beside Scripture.

03

Source and review discipline

Every piece of context needs a clear source trail, an honest rights lane, and human review before it reaches a reader.

04

Growing book by book

The work expands carefully, building repeatable context without filling every verse with unnecessary explanation.

05 — Guardrails

What fAIth is, and what it refuses to become.

What it is

  • A companion to reading Scripture
  • A way to keep helpful context close
  • A source-aware and review-first project
  • A tool built to help people know and follow Jesus better

What it is not

  • A replacement for the Bible
  • A pastor, counselor, or interpretation authority
  • A substitute for prayer, community, or the Holy Spirit
  • A place for AI guesses to quietly become truth
