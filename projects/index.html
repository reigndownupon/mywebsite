03 — Work rhythm

Start with a real question, then do the work.

  1. Begin with a real problem or something I genuinely need to learn.
  2. Ask who the work is for and why it matters before choosing the tool.
  3. Build enough to find the gaps between what I thought and what is true.
  4. Write down what worked, what failed, and what still needs care.
  5. Share it when it can save somebody else time or help them take their next step.
04 — Project boundaries
