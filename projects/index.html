03 — Work rhythm

How projects should earn their place here.

  1. Start with a real problem, learning question, or article worth responding to.
  2. Write down the deeper human issue before jumping to the tool.
  3. Build or study enough to make the work concrete.
  4. Document what changed, what was learned, and what still needs care.
  5. Share it when it can help someone else think or practice more faithfully.
04 — Project boundaries

Useful work needs clear expectations.

What belongs here

  • Projects with working notes or lessons
  • Security study connected to practical protection
  • AI experiments with clear limits
  • Learning paths that can help another learner

What does not

  • Empty project shells
  • Hype without accountability
  • Claims beyond what has been tested
  • Work that forgets the people it affects
