02 — Good reasons to reach out

Faith, learning, and practical help all belong here.

Good topics

  • Faith and encouragement
  • AI experiments or writing workflows
  • Cybersecurity questions and article ideas
  • Projects that serve people more faithfully

Less helpful

  • Hype with no practical question
  • Requests that ignore privacy or consent
  • Security shortcuts that put people at risk
  • Advice that should come from a pastor, counselor, or lawyer
