01 — About Daily Jesus

This does not need to be complicated.

Open the Bible, slow down for a minute, and pay attention to what is there. Daily Jesus is a small place to notice Jesus in Scripture and carry one faithful thing with you into the rest of the day.

02 — The format

A verse, a short description, and a few takeaways.

Each entry

Read carefully, notice Jesus, and take one faithful step.

Each entry begins with the Bible, explains the passage in plain language, and ends with something you can pray, practice, or carry into the day.
